<div class="intro-box">
**关于“生态水动力学”** —— 这里的“生态”指农业生态系统，“水动力学”是我们的方法内核。课题组以水动力学为方法、以农业生态系统为对象，研究水在其中如何运动、如何与作物和生物相互作用，并把这种理解转化为智能感知装备与精准调控技术。

生态水动力学课题组（Eco-Hydro Group）由中国农业大学徐云成副教授负责，形成“机理解析→智能感知→精准调控→系统评价”的完整研究体系。

*In our group name, "eco" refers to agricultural ecosystems, while "hydrodynamics" is our methodological core. We study how water moves within agricultural ecosystems and interacts with crops and organisms, and translate that understanding into intelligent sensing equipment and precision regulation technology.*

### 研究方向 Interests

- **农业水动力学与数值模拟** — 输配水管网、灌水器与生物污损界面的水动力机理，CDEM-OpenFOAM 耦合与基础冲刷计算程序开发。  
  **Agricultural hydrodynamics & numerical simulation** — flow mechanisms in water distribution networks, emitters and biofouling interfaces; CDEM-OpenFOAM coupling and scour modeling tools.
  <br><span style="font-size: 0.88rem; opacity: 0.75;"><em>方法内核：水动力学本身如何描述水与生物、泥沙的相互作用。</em></span>

- **智慧农田感知与无人系统** — 四足机器狗自主导航、RGB-D 视觉杂草识别、农田移动式原位监测。  
  **Smart farmland sensing & unmanned systems** — autonomous navigation for robotic dogs, RGB-D weed identification, mobile in-situ monitoring.
  <br><span style="font-size: 0.88rem; opacity: 0.75;"><em>感知手段：用无人系统读取农业生态系统中的水、土、作物信息。</em></span>

- **精准灌溉与水肥一体化智能装备** — 大型喷灌机、变量灌溉控制阀、水肥混合运移、智能决策与精准执行系统。  
  **Precision irrigation & fertigation equipment** — center-pivot, variable-rate control valves, water-fertilizer mixing, intelligent decision systems.
  <br><span style="font-size: 0.88rem; opacity: 0.75;"><em>调控出口：把水动力学认知转化为可执行的田间调控装备。</em></span>

- **农业水土系统建模与可持续性评价** — 水-土-作物耦合模型、节水灌溉效益评估、人口老龄化影响分析。  
  **Water-soil system modeling & sustainability** — coupled water-soil-crop models, irrigation benefit assessment, aging-population impact analysis.
  <br><span style="font-size: 0.88rem; opacity: 0.75;"><em>系统尺度：在区域与跨国尺度上评价水动力过程的生态与经济社会响应。</em></span>

### 合作机会 Collaboration

期待在以下交叉领域与多学科同仁、企业开展合作：

We welcome collaboration with multidisciplinary researchers and industry partners:

- **无人农场与机器人作业**：机器狗多机协同、自主巡检，杂草病虫害智能识别与靶向作业。<br>**Unmanned farms & robotics**: multi-robot coordination, autonomous inspection, intelligent weed/pest targeting.
- **数字孪生与精准灌溉**：喷灌机与微喷带水肥运移数字孪生平台，变量灌溉仿真与数据驱动决策。<br>**Digital twins & precision irrigation**: water-fertilizer digital twins for sprinkler/micro-sprinkler systems, data-driven decision models.
- **区域水-能源-粮食-生态协同**：变化环境下人口-灌溉-能源-粮食耦合模拟，节水技术效益评估与适应性政策。<br>**Regional water-energy-food-ecology nexus**: coupled population-irrigation-energy-food simulation, adaptive policy analysis.
